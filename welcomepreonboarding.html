Good to know

Questions, answered.

What is pre-onboarding?
It's everything that happens between accepting your offer and your first day. The goal is simple: help you feel informed, welcome, and ready — so Day 1 is exciting instead of overwhelming.
What do I need to prepare before Day 1?
Just work through your checklist above. It covers your signed contract, government requirements, employee information, and a few things to read. Tick each item as you go.
What should I wear on my first day?
Smart casual works well for most first days. If your role or team has a specific dress code, your manager or People & Culture contact will let you know in advance.
I have a question that isn't here. Who do I ask?
Reach out to People & Culture any time using the contact details below. No question is too small — we're here to make this easy for you.
What happens on my actual first day?
You'll be welcomed by People & Culture, go through orientation, get a tour, and meet your team. We keep the pace gentle so you can settle in comfortably.
